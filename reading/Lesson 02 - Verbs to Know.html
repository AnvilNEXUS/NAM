Extra reading · Mai's Robot (cùng 15 từ, luyện thêm)

Mai's Robot

Mai had three days to finish her robot for the science fair, but she could not concentrate. Her little brother Tuan kept knocking on her door, and the noise irritated her. She decided to consult her grandfather, a retired engineer. Everyone in the family referred to him whenever something broke.

"Grandpa, I can't manage to finish anything," Mai said. "Tuan won't leave me alone, so I reject every idea he gives me."

Grandpa smiled. "Why not let him help? Kids love to feel useful." Mai doubted it, but she coaxed Tuan into her room with a promise of cookies.

Together they assembled the robot's arms and legs. Tuan held the screws while Mai tightened them. His help boosted her speed, and soon the robot could walk a few steps. They devoted the whole afternoon to testing it.

On the first test, the robot bumped a table leg, and a stack of books toppled onto the floor. Then its battery weakened, and the robot stopped in the middle of the hallway. Tuan laughed so hard that Mai laughed too.

On the day of the fair, they transported the robot to school in a wagon. When it won second prize, Mai turned to her brother. "I apologize for shutting you out," she said. "Working with you revealed something to me: two heads really are better than one."

    Word Learning Tip

    A verb shows an action or a feeling. It often comes right after a noun or pronoun, may end in -s, -ed, -ing, and may follow a helping word such as can, will, must, could.
